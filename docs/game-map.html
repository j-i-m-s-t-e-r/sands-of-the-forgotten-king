Sands of the Forgotten King

A plan of all fifty-one rooms, with what happens in each. Hover over a room, or tab to it, to see what it involves; tap on a phone. Every secret is on this page, so it is a reference for the author and not for a first play.

The plan follows the game as built: 1000 points in all, four ways to die, two endings. Compass letters in a room's description are the game's own; the lines on the map are schematic, so rooms that overlap in the game's geography are drawn apart.
